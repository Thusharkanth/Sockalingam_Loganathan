About

Decades of Trusted Expertise
in Gold & Banking

With over 40 years of experience in Sri Lanka's banking and gold loan industry, Sockalingam Loganathan is one of the country's foremost authorities in gold valuation, authentication, and pawning operations management.

Currently serving as Pawning Inspector at DFCC Bank, he conducts island-wide audits, delivers training programmes for pawning officers, and identifies counterfeit gold articles through advanced visual and image-based analysis — often before machine testing is required.

Recognized for exceptional operational discipline, he has been personally appreciated by Central Bank of Sri Lanka audit officials for maintaining clean records and strong compliance standards throughout his career.
